# Voice AI in Firms: A Natural Field Experiment on Automated Job Interviews
**Authors:** Brian Jabarian, Luca Henkel
**Date Published:** 2026-07-30
**Link:** http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28222v1

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement
- **Core challenge:** When companies interview people for jobs, human recruiters often conduct interviews differently from one person to the next. This inconsistency can lead to unfair or poor hiring decisions.
- **Why it is important:** Collecting information accurately and fairly during interviews is crucial for organizations to hire the best talent without bias or random variations.

## 🏗️ Architectural Overview & Technical Design
- **System breakdown:** The researchers studied the use of automated "AI voice agents" designed to conduct job interviews with applicants.
- **Data flow:** The AI speaks with the applicant, asking questions and collecting information. The AI adapts its responses to the applicant while keeping the overall structure of the interview the same. The collected information is then passed to a human recruiter.
- **Technologies:** The system relies on conversational AI (voice bots) that can understand human speech and respond in real-time.

## 🛠️ Solution Approaches & Methodology
- **Implementation steps:** The researchers ran a large-scale real-world experiment involving 70,000 job applicants. Applicants were randomly chosen to be interviewed either by a human recruiter or an AI voice agent.
- **Evaluation:** After the interviews, human recruiters reviewed the collected information and made the final hiring decisions. The researchers then analyzed the interview transcripts and the hiring outcomes.
- **Key metrics:** Applicants interviewed by AI were 12% more likely to get a job offer. These hires also stayed at their jobs longer, with no drop in how well they performed their work.

## 🌍 Societal Impact & Sector Benefits
- **Industry benefit:** This research shows that AI can help human resources (HR) departments make fairer and more standardized hiring decisions.
- **Real-world use cases:** Real-world applications include using AI to handle initial job interviews for large companies, ensuring that every applicant gets a fair, structured chance to present their skills while helping companies find better matching employees.
