# Evo2Team: When Do Evolved Skills Transfer? From Selection to Deployment
**Authors:** Renxiang Wang, Jiaming Cui
**Date Published:** 2026-09-28
**Link:** http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34135v1

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement
- A skill bank that helps one multi-agent system may leave another's behavior unchanged.
- A transferred rule helps only when target agents act on it successfully.

## 🏗️ Architectural Overview & Technical Design
- We study this path for routing and communication skills in Count-Frequency and AgentsNet, using teams of 4--32 agents and GPT and Qwen model ladders.
- Source evolution meets a joint quality, cost, model-tier, and confirmation goal in 14 of 16 settings.
- Graphs and model responses change together in this comparison.

## 🛠️ Solution Approaches & Methodology
- We then evaluate Evo2Team, which selects, adapts, and confirms source skills for the target team, alongside six frozen selectors across 28 transfer directions.
- Twenty of 28 held-out outcomes meet the positive-transfer criterion, including three saved diagnostic tests.

## 🌍 Societal Impact & Sector Benefits
- By addressing the challenges mentioned, this research enhances the capabilities of AI and software engineering systems.
- Practical applications include more robust and efficient automated tools for developers.
