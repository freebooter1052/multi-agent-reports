## 🎯 Problem Statement
- As LLM agents increasingly collaborate on complex tasks, how to organize their interactions becomes a central design question.
- Existing multi-agent systems typically learn or adapt explicit roles, hierarchies, routing policies, or communication topologies.

## 🏗️ Architectural Overview & Technical Design
- As LLM agents increasingly collaborate on complex tasks, how to organize their interactions becomes a central design question.
- We introduce Waggle, a shared anonymous policy over bounded local views that jointly selects task actions, semantic communication, and local commitment updates.
