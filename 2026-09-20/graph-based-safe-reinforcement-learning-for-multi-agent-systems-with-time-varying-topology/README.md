# Graph-Based Safe Reinforcement Learning for Multi-Agent Systems with Time-Varying Topology
**Authors:** Xiao Sizhe, Dong Lijing, Bai Rui, Tan Xin
**Date Published:** 2026-09-08
**Link:** http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08802v1

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement
- To address the critical challenge of ensuring safety in environments with sensing constraints, a safety-decoupled mechanism is introduced through a Control Barrier-Like Function (CBLF) action screening layer.
- This mechanism bridges the gap between discrete LiDAR perception and continuous safety constraints, ensuring that physical safety constraints are strictly satisfied regardless of the learning progress.

## 🏗️ Architectural Overview & Technical Design
- This paper presents a graph-based safe multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL) framework for cooperative navigation with time-varying topology.
- To address the critical challenge of ensuring safety in environments with sensing constraints, a safety-decoupled mechanism is introduced through a Control Barrier-Like Function (CBLF) action screening layer.
- Building upon this safety foundation, a unified structural architecture is proposed, integrating a attention-based actor and a Graph Attention Network (GAT) centralized critic.

## 🛠️ Solution Approaches & Methodology
- The actor utilizes a value vector reconstruction mechanism that explicitly encodes relative geometric relations through a collaborative tracking error matrix, enabling scale-insensitive policy learning under time-varying communication topologies.
