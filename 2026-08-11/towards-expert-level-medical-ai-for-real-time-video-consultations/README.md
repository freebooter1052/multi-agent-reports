## 🎯 Problem Statement
- Limitations remain in fine anatomical precision, subtle affective nuances, and high-frequency movements.

## 🏗️ Architectural Overview & Technical Design
- In a randomized Objective Structured Clinical Examination (OSCE) study with 30 primary care physicians (PCPs), 15 patient actors and 100 clinical scenarios, we compared AMIE (Video), its text-only counterpart AMIE (Text), and PCPs consulting via video.

## 🛠️ Solution Approaches & Methodology
- Patient actors preferred AMIE's approach to assessing and explaining conditions, while PCPs were preferred for rapport and partnership building.

## 🌍 Societal Impact & Sector Benefits
- While further research is needed before real-world translation, these results mark an important milestone toward AI systems capable of augmenting care across the sensory complexity of clinical practice..
