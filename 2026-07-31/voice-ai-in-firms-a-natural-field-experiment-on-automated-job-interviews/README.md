# Voice AI in Firms: A Natural Field Experiment on Automated Job Interviews
**Authors:** Brian Jabarian, Luca Henkel
**Date Published:** 2026-07-30
**Link:** http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28222v1

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement
- Human recruiters can be inconsistent when interviewing job applicants, leading to high variation in how candidate information is collected and evaluated.
- This is a significant challenge because inconsistent interviews make it difficult for companies to make fair and accurate hiring decisions.

## 🏗️ Architectural Overview & Technical Design
- The study investigates the use of automated AI voice agents to conduct initial job interviews instead of human recruiters.
- The system involves the AI agent asking structured questions to the candidate, listening to their responses, and adapting to the conversation while maintaining consistency.
- The conversational data (interview transcripts) is then analyzed by human recruiters who make the final hiring decisions based on the information collected.

## 🛠️ Solution Approaches & Methodology
- The researchers conducted a large-scale field experiment with 70,000 job applicants, randomly assigning them to be interviewed either by a human or an AI voice agent.
- They analyzed the interview transcripts to see how structured and consistent the AI interviews were compared to the human ones.
- They evaluated the results by tracking key metrics, including the percentage of applicants who received job offers, how many actually started the job, and how long they stayed with the company.

## 🌍 Societal Impact & Sector Benefits
- This research benefits the human resources sector by demonstrating that standardizing information collection with AI can lead to better and fairer hiring decisions.
- A practical real-world application is the widespread deployment of AI interviewers to conduct preliminary job screenings, which could increase efficiency and potentially reduce human bias in early hiring stages.
