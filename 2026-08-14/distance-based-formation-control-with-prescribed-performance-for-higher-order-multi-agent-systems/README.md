# Distance-Based Formation Control with Prescribed Performance for Higher-Order Multi-Agent Systems
**Authors:** Janina Schaa, Maria Charitidou, Dimos Dimarogonas, Thomas Berger
**Date Published:** 2026-08-13
**Link:** http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12896v1

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement
- We consider distributed distance-based formation control with prescribed transient performance for multi-agent systems modeled by unknown nonlinear dynamics of relative degree greater than one.
- We introduce a virtual leader whose trajectory is tracked with prescribed transient behavior by a designated subset of agents.

## 🏗️ Architectural Overview & Technical Design
- We consider distributed distance-based formation control with prescribed transient performance for multi-agent systems modeled by unknown nonlinear dynamics of relative degree greater than one.
- We introduce a virtual leader whose trajectory is tracked with prescribed transient behavior by a designated subset of agents.
- In each setting, a distributed and model-free control law is developed, and we establish the satisfaction of the prescribed funnel constraints on the formation and tracking errors, and boundedness of all closed-loop signals.
