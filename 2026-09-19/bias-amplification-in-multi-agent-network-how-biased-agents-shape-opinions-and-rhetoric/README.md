# Bias Amplification in Multi-Agent Network: How Biased Agents Shape Opinions and Rhetoric
**Authors:** Omran Berjawi, Giuseppe Fenza, Rida Khatoun
**Date Published:** 2026-09-16
**Link:** http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18306v1

## 🎯 Problem Statement
- Despite significant research into the functioning of LLMs in such multi-agent systems, the processes of bias propagation in such systems are still a challenge.

## 🏗️ Architectural Overview & Technical Design
- Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly deployed in applications involving interaction between agents, where their output plays a role in collective reasoning and decision-making processes.
- This work studies how biased opinions are propagated in the form of textual interaction in an environment of LLMs, in which a minority of agents maintain persistent extreme opinions, while the remaining agents iteratively update their beliefs through structured textual interactions.
- It suggests that for the same percentage of biased agents, the shifts occur more quickly for the Llama~3.2 model when compared to a classical Friedkin-Johnsen (FJ) model.
